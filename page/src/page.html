How the city reads
Every herdr workspace is a district, and every agent in a pane is a robot (name from herdr, badge from the CLI/model). Cyan eyes mean working; amber plus a "Needs you" card means blocked on a prompt; idle 10 minutes or more dims the district; error text on the pane turns the robot red. P0 is read-only: answer prompts in the herdr pane. Approvals from the city are a later phase.
